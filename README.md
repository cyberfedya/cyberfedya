# Hi, I'm cyberfedya 👋

I build things end to end: mobile, web and backend.

## What I'm working on

**My Vocab** — an English vocabulary app that turns *your own* words into everything you practise:

- 🎮 Duolingo-style games made from your dictionary (lesson with hearts, match pairs, spelling, listening, blitz, memory)
- 📞 A video call with a 3D AI tutor that talks only in English, adapts to your level and corrects your mistakes
- 🔁 Spaced repetition (SM-2) so words are reviewed right before you forget them
- 🌍 Russian and Uzbek interface

The repositories are private for now.
